Pricing Policy – Europe Luxury Retail

Standard discounting is not permitted for core collection products.
Any exception request must be approved by the regional commercial director and justified by one of the following conditions:
1. end-of-season stock clearance,
2. damaged packaging with intact product quality,
3. VIP retention case with documented revenue history,
4. local event-driven traffic activation approved in advance.

Discount exceptions above 10% require finance review.
Repeated discounting in the same boutique within a 60-day period must be escalated.
The objective is to protect brand equity while allowing controlled flexibility in exceptional cases.
