## Week 6 – Scenario Simulation Layer

### Objective
Extend the decision engine from static forecasting to scenario-based decision intelligence.

### What was built
- Marketing uplift simulation
- Supply shock simulation
- Adjusted forecast calculation
- Dynamic decision signal based on simulated conditions

### Executive value
Enables decision-makers to test "what-if" scenarios and anticipate operational impacts before execution.

### Key insight
Decisions should not rely on static forecasts, but on simulated outcomes under different strategic assumptions.
